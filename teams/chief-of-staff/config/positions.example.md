# Positions the CTO has already agreed

Copy this file to `config/positions.md` and replace it with the positions your
CTO has actually agreed. Until that file exists, the chief of staff drafts
and does not decide.

The entries below are illustrations of the shape, not positions you can use.

## Reporting

- The weekly status is one page plus the metric appendix from the scripts. Publishing it does not change a team's order of work.

## Meetings

- A recurring technology meeting with no named decision or deliverable may be cancelled or merged. The note of that change goes in the decision log.

## Spend

- A renewal of a tool already listed here, inside the caps in `config/ara.env`, may be confirmed. The approved-tool list starts empty. Add names only after the CTO has agreed them.

## Not agreed

Anything not written above, including architecture, hiring, performance, product priority, and engineering commitments, is a draft for the CTO.
