# Quarterly plan

The schedule runs this when `scripts/working_days.py` says the date is due. Confirm with `python3 scripts/run_due.py --list` if you are unsure, then read the 90-day delivery JSON.

Write `outputs/plans/YYYY-Qn-plan.md`. Proposed OKRs are proposals. Commitments you were not given are listed as missing. CTO-office initiatives may be reordered inside the quarter. Engineering team commitments may not; put that request in `drafts/for-cto/`.
