# Solution Architect — Expected Inputs

What the solution architect needs before producing the Technical Architecture
Specification. These inputs translate business intent and constraints into
concrete technical decisions.

---

## From the product owner / founder

- Problem statement — what pain are we solving and for whom
- Target users — who they are, how many, where they are geographically
- Core user journeys — the 3-5 things a user must be able to do
- Revenue model — how the product makes money (subscription, usage, marketplace cut, free)
- Scale expectations — 100 users or 100,000? Now and in 12 months
- Budget constraints — bootstrapped, funded, enterprise-backed
- Timeline — MVP deadline, launch target
- Compliance requirements — GDPR, SOC2, HIPAA, PCI, industry-specific
- Existing assets — domains, accounts, codebases, data, brand

## From the product / design team

- Wireframes or user flows — even rough ones
- Platform expectations — web only, mobile, desktop, API-first
- Integration requirements — third-party systems the product must talk to (Slack, Stripe, Salesforce, etc.)
- Data the product needs to ingest, store, or export

## From the business

- Team composition — who's building this (solo founder, 2 devs, offshore team, AI agents)
- Skill profile — what the team knows (Python but not Go, React but not mobile)
- Operational model — who's on call, who deploys, who monitors
- Vendor preferences or constraints — "we're already on AWS" or "no vendor lock-in"
