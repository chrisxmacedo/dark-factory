01

Indicators

Each key result references a tracked indicator — a categorical measure that changes over time as the product is deployed to the target market. These indicators are plotted over time to evidence progress toward objectives.

Registered Users
Active Workspaces
Daily Active Users
Session Duration
Monthly Recurring Revenue
Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate
Pipeline Visibility Score
Bottleneck Detection Rate
Monthly Churn Rate
Integration Connections
Net Promoter Score
VR Session Adoption Rate
  • All indicators are measured continuously and plotted over time on a separate tracking dashboard
  • Key results state the target change (% increase, % decrease, or absolute value) in a specific indicator
  • Objectives are timeboxed to quarters — Q1 targets early adoption, Q2–Q3 growth, Q4 revenue and retention
  • Baseline values are established at product launch; all targets are measured from baseline
02

O1: Market Adoption

O1
Establish the VR Pipeline Dashboard as the default pipeline visualisation tool for AI-augmented development teams
Q1 – Q2
  • KR 1.1
    Grow the number of registered users from launch baseline to demonstrate product-market demand within the primary target segment of AI-augmented engineering teams.
    ● Registered Users ↑ +500 users
  • KR 1.2
    Increase the number of active workspaces (teams with at least one connected integration running for 7+ days) to validate that adoption extends beyond individual trial to team-level use.
    ● Active Workspaces ↑ +100 workspaces
  • KR 1.3
    Achieve a Net Promoter Score above the threshold that indicates organic growth potential, measured through in-app surveys after 14 days of active use.
    ● Net Promoter Score ≥ NPS 40
03

O2: User Engagement

O2
Prove that the spatial pipeline view delivers sustained daily value, not novelty — users return because it is genuinely useful
Q2 – Q3
  • KR 2.1
    Increase the ratio of daily active users to registered users, evidencing that the product is part of the daily workflow rather than a one-time demo.
    ● Daily Active Users ↑ +40% DAU/MAU ratio
  • KR 2.2
    Increase average session duration, demonstrating that users spend meaningful time observing the pipeline rather than briefly checking and leaving.
    ● Session Duration ↑ +25% avg duration
  • KR 2.3
    Grow the proportion of sessions initiated in VR mode (headset) versus desktop-only, indicating that the immersive experience delivers additional value worth the friction of putting on a headset.
    ● VR Session Adoption Rate ↑ +15% VR sessions
  • KR 2.4
    Reduce monthly churn rate to demonstrate that once teams adopt the product, they retain it as an ongoing part of their workflow.
    ● Monthly Churn Rate ↓ <5% monthly churn
04

O3: Revenue

O3
Build a sustainable revenue base that validates willingness to pay for spatial pipeline observability
Q3 – Q4
  • KR 3.1
    Grow monthly recurring revenue from paid team and enterprise subscriptions, evidencing that the product delivers enough value to justify a recurring cost.
    ● Monthly Recurring Revenue ↑ £5,000 MRR
  • KR 3.2
    Increase the conversion rate from free individual tier to paid team tier, demonstrating that team-level features and shared spaces are compelling enough to drive upgrades.
    ● Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate ↑ +8% conversion rate
  • KR 3.3
    Reduce monthly churn among paying customers to below the threshold that supports compounding revenue growth over time.
    ● Monthly Churn Rate ↓ <3% paid churn
05

O4: Product Effectiveness

O4
Demonstrate that the spatial view measurably improves pipeline visibility and reduces the time to detect bottlenecks
Q2 – Q4
  • KR 4.1
    Increase the self-reported pipeline visibility score (in-app survey: “How confident are you that you know the current state of your pipeline?” 1–10), measured before and after adoption.
    ● Pipeline Visibility Score ↑ +60% avg score
  • KR 4.2
    Increase the rate at which bottlenecks (stuck builds, failed deploys, stalled PRs) are detected within 15 minutes of occurrence, as measured by time-to-first-action after a blockage event.
    ● Bottleneck Detection Rate ↑ +50% detection within 15min
  • KR 4.3
    Increase the average number of connected integrations per workspace, evidencing that teams derive more value from a unified view spanning multiple data sources.
    ● Integration Connections ↑ +3 avg integrations/workspace
06

O5: Platform Expansion

O5
Expand the integration ecosystem to cover the majority of tools used by target market teams, reducing friction to adoption
Q3 – Q4
  • KR 5.1
    Increase the total number of supported integration connectors (project management, CI/CD, deployment, AI agent platforms) to cover the top tools used by the target market.
    ● Integration Connections ≥ 10 supported integrations
  • KR 5.2
    Increase the number of active workspaces, driven by reduced friction from broader integration support making the product accessible to teams using non-Jira/GitHub stacks.
    ● Active Workspaces ↑ +80% workspaces QoQ
  • KR 5.3
    Grow registered users through organic adoption driven by word-of-mouth and open-source community engagement.
    ● Registered Users ↑ +200% users QoQ
